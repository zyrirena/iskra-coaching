I

Irena brings systems thinking to human potential.

She has seen from the inside how careers are built, stalled, and rebuilt, and what separates people who move forward deliberately from those who drift.

With a background in enterprise-level HR, Irena understands how hiring, promotion, and talent decisions really happen. Years of system optimization taught her a simple discipline: find the constraint, test a change, measure the result. She applies it to people with the same rigor, and far more care.

Her background in responsible AI keeps her attentive to fairness, transparency, and the human consequences of the systems we all work within.

Clients find a rare pairing: deeply empathetic listening alongside a clear, analytical mind. Irena will not tell you what to do. She will help you see your situation clearly, then build a plan you can stand behind.
